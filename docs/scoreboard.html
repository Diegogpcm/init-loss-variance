Lower is better. A certificate is an upper bound on the true test error that holds with high probability; the test error is shown for scale.

MNIST, n = 60,000

Best data-free result we know of: 0.0648, the prequential AIS certificate on second-order scattering features (test error 0.015). With Gabor features it gives 0.0726 on both seeds, and the floating-point-free exact sampler reproduces that (0.0732 and 0.0716). Our earlier best was 0.0695 (DP-SGD + mutual information).

Fashion-MNIST, n = 55,000

We know of no published full-data, data-free, 10-class Fashion certificate to compare against; Valle-Pérez et al. (2019) report 0.175–0.188 on a binarised 10k subset, which is not comparable. The prequential AIS certificate gives 0.2206 with the exact sampler (test error 0.126), ahead of our DP-SGD 0.249.

CIFAR-10, n = 50,000

Ours ties the best published data-free certificate (Lampert, Cairney-Leeming & Zakerinia 2026, 0.716 ± 0.007) and beats García-Pérez et al. 2025 (0.738), using first-order Gabor features with no tuning on CIFAR. The AIS method has not been run on CIFAR yet.

Routes tried that did not improve a certificate

  • Structured multi-epoch tail for DP-SGD (theory.md §14.2): worth at most 0.008 at the measured gradient diversity; dropped.
  • Individual (Rényi-filter) privacy accounting (§14.3): no gain; the filter drops misclassified points first.
  • Chaos as self-generated noise (§15.4, Fashion): fixed and random seeds give the same function-space information. Explanatory, not a certificate.
  • Time-uniform information via contraction (Altschuler–Talwar, §16): best Fashion certificate 0.321 against 0.249; forgetting time equals optimisation time.
  • SMC particles for the AIS certificate (§17): 8 particles help by at most 3% (0.1665 → 0.161 on pixels, 0.0726 → 0.0705 on Gabor features).
  • Wider head (300 units on Gabor features): 0.080 against 0.0726.
  • CNN on pixels under HMC (n = 5000 pilots): learns no faster than the Gabor MLP; step-size tuning still running.
